Examples#

Six runnable examples: two score responses with a published detector, three train a detector of your own, and one scores live Langfuse traces. Installation is in Installation and the scikit-learn behaviour the notebooks rely on is in scikit-learn compatibility; the training workflow is in Training a detector.
